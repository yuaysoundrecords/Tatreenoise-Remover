Tatree Noise Remover 2 — Mac helper

Install the plug-in first. Close your DAW and plug-in scanners.
If macOS blocks Tatree VST3 and Privacy & Security approval does not work,
you may use Approve-Tatree.command only if you trust this download source.

Double-click the helper, review the target paths, and type YES to confirm.
Enter your Mac password if prompted; typing is hidden.
After Done, open your DAW and rescan Tatree.
If the plug-in is missing or an error occurs, stop and contact the developer.

This removes quarantine only from Tatree VST3, not AU or Standalone.
It does not disable Gatekeeper globally and is not Apple notarization.
macOS may block the helper itself. Use Privacy & Security to approve only files you trust.
Signature verification checks file integrity, not publisher identity.
